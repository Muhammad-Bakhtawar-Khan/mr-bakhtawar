# 90-day roadmap (no outcome guarantees)

## Days 1–14: establish correct public foundations

Publish the validated static build to the existing origin. Recheck every route,
redirect, image and sitemap on production. Verify Search Console ownership and
submit the canonical sitemap. Compare rendered and HTML-only homepage content.
Owner reviews social identities, actual service scope and public project claims.

## Days 15–30: collect a real demand baseline

Export Search Console queries/pages/countries/devices, indexing diagnostics and
field CWV where available. Separate personal-brand searches from unrelated
namesakes. Obtain a reliable localized competitor export. Confirm whether
ecommerce and any foreign markets merit expanded content.

## Days 31–60: strengthen evidence, not page count

Document two real projects with screenshots, roles, architecture decisions,
limitations and permission to publish. Improve existing short guides only where
they answer real questions. Update GitHub/DEV profiles with owner approval;
publish an original technical write-up and link naturally to supporting work.

## Days 61–90: measure and refine

Compare 28-day windows with seasonality and deployment annotations. Review
branded impressions/clicks, relevant enquiries, coverage, duplicate canonicals and
field UX. Refresh pages with demonstrated unmet intent. Repeat the regression
audit before each release. Defer unsupported markets and specializations.

Search indexing, rich results, knowledge panels and AI citations remain external
decisions; no deadline or position is promised.
