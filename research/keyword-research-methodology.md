# Keyword evidence and limits

The master contains **191 deduplicated opportunities**: 22 records traceable to
observed autocomplete suggestions and 169 owner/source-supported candidates.
These categories are explicitly distinguished in every row. No record has a
measured search volume, difficulty or CPC; these cells are blank, not zero.

Autocomplete requests used English and a Pakistan country hint. A returned
phrase is evidence of a suggestion, not monthly searches, a forecast, a country
distribution or proof of identity. Branded suggestions remain ambiguous until
Search Console or reliable SERPs confirm intent.

Other candidates come from explicit user-supplied brand terms, distinct existing
page topics, visible FAQ questions and existing portrait assets. They are
content/identity opportunities, **not verified search-demand measurements**.
Every row has a source, collection date, confidence label and target URL.

Thirty-seven irrelevant suggestions were excluded with reasons. No combinations
of city × service × adjective were generated to reach an arbitrary target.
Shopify specialization and Gulf offices/customers are not substantiated. Existing
ecommerce/catalogue scope supports a few relevant suggestions, not a new claim
of platform expertise or international delivery.

The 10,000 objective is not achieved: legitimate available evidence does not
justify 10,000 distinct useful records. The remaining research needs GSC exports,
Keyword Planner or another attributable dataset plus review. Do not describe all
191 records as observed queries or demand-verified keywords.

Normalization uses Unicode NFKC, case folding and whitespace cleanup. The owner's
explicit `Mr.` punctuation variant is retained separately as a legitimate brand
spelling. Target URL aliases are mapped to their final canonical destination.

CSV metric provenance is the Source/Research Date/Evidence Confidence fields.
There are no observed numeric demand metrics requiring a separate metric date.
Priority is a disclosed editorial heuristic, not a Google or vendor metric.
