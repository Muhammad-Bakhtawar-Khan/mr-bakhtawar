04 DATA UI

MAKING INFORMATION LEGIBLE

A market-data interface, not financial advice

MBK Markets presents information for a cryptocurrency catalogue (coverage depends on the current data source), helping visitors browse a wide range of digital assets from one place. The interface is designed to make market information easier to scan, compare and explore instead of overwhelming visitors with an unstructured list of numbers.

Charts depend on their data sources

Interactive charts include candlestick-style views that help users inspect price movements and market trends over time. Live values and chart details depend on the connected data source and its update status, so users should check the displayed timeframe and source before relying on a figure.

What still needs verification

The project explores data presentation, responsive layout and chart-driven market analysis. It is an informational tool, not financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any asset.
