NEW SEO / DISCOVERY

PRACTICAL NOTES / BAKHTAWAR KHAN

Give each important page a descriptive title, a concise page-specific meta description and a clear main heading. Use meaningful internal links so people and crawlers can discover projects, services, articles and contact details without relying only on interactive controls.

Check that the canonical URL matches the preferred public URL, that the sitemap contains real canonical pages and that robots rules do not block important content. Inspect the rendered page, response status and indexability in Search Console. A sitemap helps discovery; it does not guarantee indexing or rankings.

Write articles that answer a real question and include your own explanation, examples, limitations and sources when relevant. Avoid creating dozens of near-identical keyword pages or stuffing meta keywords. Keep images compressed and give meaningful images useful alt text.

After publishing, inspect important URLs, fix broken links and monitor Search Console for indexing issues. Rankings depend on competition, content quality, links, user intent and many other factors, so avoid guarantees.
