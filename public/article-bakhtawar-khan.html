01 PROFILE

A PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

The person behind the portfolio

I’m Bakhtawar Khan, also known online as MBK. I’m interested in web development, practical AI tools, APIs, automation and the craft of making digital experiences feel clear and useful. My approach is to learn through small projects: plan a simple idea, build an interface, test how it behaves and keep improving it.

A record of practical work

This website brings my projects, writing, visual work and online profiles together in one place. It is a work in progress by design. I want it to show not only finished links, but also the process behind them: what I’m exploring, what I’ve learned and what I want to make better next.

Learning without inflated promises

My focus is on steady progress rather than pretending to know everything. I value practical learning, honest descriptions and the kind of collaboration where a clear problem leads to a useful result.
