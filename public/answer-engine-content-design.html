Start with a genuine question

Answer-ready content begins with a question users actually ask, followed by a direct answer and supporting context. Use descriptive headings, short explanations, relevant links and consistent entity names. For questions about Bakhtawar Khan (MBK), distinguish personal identity from MBK Global and from the separate websites of associated projects. Avoid duplicate filler, unsupported credentials and promises of guaranteed inclusion in answer engines.

Keep authorship and evidence visible
